# 全新故障艺术视觉概念

本轮按“全部推翻重新设计”重做四张静态视觉稿，先供用户选择；不是运行中的网页截图，也不代表已实现动效或播放器功能。正式放映厅未改动。

[打开四版对比页](index.html)。四份定稿均为 1536 × 1024 PNG，尺寸、PNG 校验与唯一性已核对，内容清单见 [manifest.json](manifest.json)。本目录只保留本轮四张定稿，生成首稿由定稿原位替换。

| 编号 | 方案     | 天空与视觉方向               | 设计图                          |
| ---- | -------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| A    | 天光断层 | 晴昼、瓷白与钴蓝、建筑式构图 | [查看](01-daylight-rupture.png) |
| B    | 暮色熔帧 | 落日、暖铜与深紫、错位浮岛   | [查看](02-dusk-datamosh.png)    |
| C    | 风暴碎页 | 风暴、石墨与银白、厚片撕裂   | [查看](03-storm-fracture.png)   |
| D    | 极夜回声 | 极夜、墨色与薄荷、极光拖影   | [查看](04-aurora-echo.png)      |

共通要求：写实天空照片基底、强烈横向像素排序、水平撕裂与拉伸、材质碎片与数据熔合、零散漂浮像素、轻微 RGB 色偏。按钮和容器保持可辨认的整体轮廓、完整中心与清晰文字；电影画面保持干净。四张采用不同构图、材料、光线、颜色及破碎方式，不以旧 B 版换色。

生成方式：内置 image_gen，四个独立生成请求，并分别定向修整；无参考旧稿、无 CLI/API 回退。本文件记录完整生成提示词，定稿修整见 [编辑提示词](EDIT-PROMPTS.md)。设计图是概念图，生成文字可能与最终排版略有不同；选定后再用真实 HTML/CSS/Canvas 实现并验证。

## A · 天光断层

保存路径：`docs/design/glitch-concepts/01-daylight-rupture.png`

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create one exceptionally art-directed, high-detail, photorealistic desktop web interface concept for VideoSreeeen, a multiplayer watch room. This is a fresh visual redesign, not a recolor of a generic dashboard. Wide landscape 3:2 image, frontal flat UI screenshot, no monitor/device frame, no perspective mockup. Typography and composition must look professionally designed and intentional. Use actual photographic sky as the full-bleed visual foundation, natural photographic detail, cinematic light. The main video player shows a calm photographic ocean-cliff movie still and stays clean, unobstructed, rectangular and readable; all damage stays outside the movie picture. Product functions visible: video, link input with a sync button, shared playback / screen sharing / local video choices, a compact text chat and a small voice control. Use very few, beautifully typeset, readable Chinese labels, exact text if shown: "共同播放", "共享画面", "本地视频", "同步链接", "聊天室", "邀请成员", "语音", "发送". Brand exact: "VideoSreeeen". A small visible note "视觉概念稿" marks this as a concept. No romantic slogan, no marketing landing page, no fake latency numbers.

MANDATORY VISUAL LANGUAGE: true GLITCH ART, very strong horizontal pixel sorting, stretched horizontal tearing, digital file corruption, beautiful datamoshing-like fusion of photographic pixels with interface material. Buttons and panel surfaces visibly fragment into many irregular horizontal slivers and substantial displaced shards concentrated near their silhouette and selected faults, with a few small detached pixel squares suspended nearby. The shards must carry the actual material/color of their parent panel and photographic samples, not merely white horizontal border lines. Irregular thickness, varying lengths, directional clusters, sheared stretched trails, subtle red/cyan RGB separation. Keep the overall button and container contour clearly identifiable; preserve broad continuous central surfaces, crisp intact typography, clear hit-target silhouettes. Build convincing broken material, not a barcode, not all-over scanlines, not dotted neon outlines, not noisy RGB static. Dense spectacular corruption can occur in the background and in select outer-edge clusters, while reading and the video remain calm. High-detail cinematic finish.

CONCEPT 01 / DAYLIGHT RUPTURE. A brilliant photoreal blue daytime sky, towering white sunlit cumulus clouds, crisp natural sunlight. Architectural porcelain-white and saturated cobalt-blue interface pieces with sharp asymmetric stepped silhouettes, beautiful polished photographic paper / brushed aluminum material, ink-blue type. Strong elegant Swiss editorial composition: large clean video at center-left, a slim vertical chat on the right, a single low horizontal source-control ribbon under the player, generous open sky above. Large confident "01" and small "DAYLIGHT RUPTURE" as design presentation title. Bold cobalt sync button with one edge disintegrating into sky-blue / porcelain horizontal slivers, several prominent torn material clusters at the card corners. Rich blue photopixels pulled into long horizontal sorting streaks in the open sky. Make this a bright, graphic, precise daytime design, not pastel generic glass cards.
```
